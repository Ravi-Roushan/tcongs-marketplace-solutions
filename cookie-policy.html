Cookie Policy

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit websites. They help analyze web traffic, remember user preferences, and improve general site functionality.

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies on our website to:

  • Track and analyze visitor traffic to optimize our content and layouts
  • Remember your preferences and form selections for a faster experience
  • Serve relevant marketing campaigns and track advertising conversions

Types of Cookies Used

1. Essential Cookies

These cookies are required for the basic operation of the website, such as managing session tokens and contact form submissions.

2. Analytics Cookies

We use tools like Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our site, helping us monitor performance and improve user experience.

3. Marketing Cookies

These cookies track your browsing behavior across websites to enable remarketing and target advertising campaigns (e.g., through Google Ads or Meta Ads).

Managing Cookies

Most browsers accept cookies automatically, but you can configure your browser settings to refuse cookies or notify you when a cookie is set. Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of certain elements on our website.
