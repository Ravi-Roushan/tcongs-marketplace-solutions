Accessibility Statement

Our Commitment

TCONGS Marketplace Solutions is committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to all individuals, including users with disabilities. We continually work to improve usability and conform to WCAG accessibility guidelines.

Accessibility Initiatives

To support accessibility, we implement the following standards:

  • High-readability sans-serif typography across all pages
  • Proper color contrast between text and background elements
  • Standard HTML headings structure for screen-reader readability
  • Responsive design optimized for zoom and mobile display
  • Clickable elements with descriptive alt labels and IDs

Ongoing Reviews

We regularly audit our code and layouts to detect and resolve accessibility barriers. Despite our efforts, some legacy content or third-party integrations may have temporary compliance issues.
